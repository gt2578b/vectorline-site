SERVICES

Advisory for leaders navigating the digitalization of human processes.

Vectorline helps organizations build trustworthy content and AI systems in a world of synthetic media and information risk. We focus on what is actually deployable in your environment, with clear guidance and practical next steps.

01

Provenance & authenticity strategy

A structured advisory engagement to design your content provenance and authenticity program, grounded in C2PA and related standards.

Outcomes include a clear view of where provenance matters in your products and workflows, a decision framework for adopting C2PA, and a phased roadmap for implementation.

WHO IT'S FOR

Platforms and media organizations

Product and policy teams responsible for trust and integrity

Standards and ecosystem leads

02

AI trust & integrity advisory

Strategic guidance for teams building or deploying AI systems that interact with content, creators, and audiences.

Outcomes include alignment of AI features with integrity and safety expectations, strategy for labeling and disclosure, and integration of provenance signals into workflows and UX.

WHO IT'S FOR

AI product teams

Trust & safety and integrity groups

Leadership teams navigating AI risk and opportunity

03

Standards & ecosystem positioning

Support for how you show up in the broader ecosystem around AI, provenance, and trust.

Outcomes include a map of relevant standards bodies and events, narrative and positioning for external engagement, and a practical plan for participation without over-committing.

WHO IT'S FOR

Companies entering the provenance and trust space

Organizations seeking a credible voice in AI and authenticity debates

Teams preparing for speaking, panels, and working groups

04

Early-stage advisory

Focused advisory support for startups and early-stage teams working on creator rights, provenance infrastructure, and AI tools for media and creative work.

Outcomes include product and ecosystem feedback grounded in standards and policy realities, plus guidance on narrative, go-to-market, and relevant communities.

WHO IT'S FOR

Founders building trust-centered products

Teams shaping provenance and authenticity infrastructure

Early-stage companies navigating AI and creator rights

HOW WE ENGAGE

Built for depth, not duration.

Vectorline engagements are scoped to deliver structural outcomes — not billable hours. We work on a retained or project basis, depending on the nature and timeline of the challenge.

Every engagement begins with an architectural assessment: a structured analysis of the problem space, stakeholder landscape, and systemic constraints. From there, we design and deliver the frameworks, models, and architectures that constitute the solution.
