# Services

## 1. Provenance & authenticity strategy

### What this is

A structured advisory engagement to design your **content provenance and authenticity program**, grounded in C2PA and related standards.

### Outcomes

- Clear view of **where provenance matters** in your products and workflows
- Decision framework for **adopting C2PA and complementary approaches**
- Roadmap for **phased implementation** across teams and partners

### Who it’s for

- Platforms and media organizations
- Product and policy teams responsible for trust and integrity
- Standards and ecosystem leads

---

## 2. AI trust & integrity advisory

### What this is

Strategic guidance for teams building or deploying AI systems that interact with content, creators, and audiences.

### Outcomes

- Alignment of AI features with **integrity and safety expectations**
- Strategy for **labeling and disclosure** of AI‑generated content
- Integration of **provenance signals** into AI workflows and UX

### Who it’s for

- AI product teams
- Trust & safety and integrity groups
- Leadership teams navigating AI risk and opportunity

---

## 3. Standards & ecosystem positioning

### What this is

Support for how you show up in the broader ecosystem around AI, provenance, and trust.

### Outcomes

- Map of **relevant standards bodies, coalitions, and events**
- Narrative and positioning for **external engagement**
- Practical plan for **participation and influence** without over‑committing

### Who it’s for

- Companies entering the provenance / trust space
- Organizations seeking a **credible voice** in AI and authenticity debates
- Teams preparing for **speaking, panels, and working groups**

---

## 4. Early‑stage advisory

### What this is

Focused advisory support for startups and early‑stage teams working on:

- Creator rights and NIL
- Provenance and authenticity infrastructure
- AI tools for media and creative work

### Outcomes

- Product and ecosystem feedback grounded in **standards and policy realities**
- Go‑to‑market and narrative guidance around **trust and authenticity**
- Connections to relevant **events, partners, and communities**
