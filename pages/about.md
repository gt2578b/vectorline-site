# About Vectorline

## Origin

Vectorline Partners was created to answer a specific problem:

> How do we build **trustworthy content and AI systems** in a world where synthetic media is cheap, fast, and everywhere?

The practice sits at the intersection of:

- **Technical standards** (like C2PA)
- **Platform and product realities**
- **Policy, regulation, and public‑interest expectations**

---

## About Andrew Jenks

### Founding chair, C2PA

Andrew served as **founding chair of C2PA**, the global standard for content provenance and authenticity.  
In that role, he helped convene and align organizations across media, technology, and standards communities to define how provenance should work at internet scale.

This experience gives Vectorline:

- Direct insight into **how provenance standards are made**
- Relationships across the **ecosystem of implementers and advocates**
- A practical sense of **what will actually gain adoption**

### Practice background

Andrew’s work spans:

- **Integrity and provenance initiatives** at large technology companies
- Collaboration with **media, creative, and standards organizations**
- Advisory roles with early‑stage companies working on trust, AI, and creator rights

Vectorline is the vehicle for bringing that experience to organizations that need focused, independent guidance.

---

## How Vectorline works

### Engagement style

Vectorline engagements are:

- **Lean:** small, senior teams; no junior staffing layers
- **Strategic:** focused on decisions, not deliverable volume
- **Collaborative:** working alongside your product, policy, and leadership teams

### Typical outcomes

Clients work with Vectorline to:

- Decide **if and how** to adopt C2PA and related standards
- Design **provenance and labeling strategies** for AI‑generated content
- Shape **external narratives** around trust and authenticity
- Prepare for **regulatory and ecosystem shifts** in AI and media

---

## Values

- **Integrity over optics:** We prioritize what’s actually defensible and sustainable.
- **Clarity over complexity:** We translate standards and policy into plain language and concrete options.
- **Human‑centered:** We care about how technology affects creators, audiences, and institutions, not just systems.

Vectorline exists to help organizations make **better, more honest decisions** about trust in the age of AI.
