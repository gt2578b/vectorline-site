# Clients & partners

Vectorline works with organizations that sit at the intersection of **content, AI, and trust**.

---

## Types of organizations

- **Technology platforms** building AI and content products
- **Media and creative organizations** navigating synthetic content and provenance
- **Standards and policy groups** shaping transparency and authenticity
- **Early‑stage companies** working on trust, creator rights, and provenance infrastructure

---

## How we engage

- **Confidential advisory** relationships with leadership teams
- **Project‑based work** around provenance, AI trust, and ecosystem strategy
- **Collaborative initiatives** with standards and multilateral organizations

If you’re working in this space and need a **clear, grounded perspective**, Vectorline is designed to be a partner, not just a vendor.
