# Vectorline Partners

## AI, provenance & trust for the real world

Vectorline is an advisory practice focused on one thing:  
helping organizations build **trustworthy content and AI systems** in a world of synthetic media and information risk.

Founded by **Andrew Jenks — founding chair of C2PA**, the global standard for content provenance and authenticity — Vectorline works with teams who need clarity, credibility, and practical implementation, not just slideware.

## What we do

### Provenance & authenticity strategy

We help you design and implement content provenance and authenticity programs that stand up to scrutiny from:

- **Regulators and policymakers**
- **Media and creative partners**
- **Internal risk, compliance, and legal teams**

From C2PA adoption to ecosystem alignment, we focus on what’s actually deployable in your environment.

We work with product, policy, and engineering leaders to:

- Align AI features with **integrity and safety expectations**

- Standards bodies and multilateral organizations
- Industry coalitions and working groups
- Conferences and public forums where trust and AI are being defined
